The app lists posts as you, with your browser's sessionid cookie. To get a fresh one: log in on instagram.com, press F12, open Application → Cookies → https://www.instagram.com and copy the Value of sessionid. It's saved in session.txt on this computer only and never shown here. Anyone with it is logged in as you.

Skipped photos

Always skipped: photos with nobody in them, and photos with people but no girl (men, crowds, stages). Add your own rules below: a photo with any of these tags is skipped too. One rule per line, name: booru_tag ... | cutoff. The booru tags come from the tagger's list (with underscores), and the cutoff (default 0.35) is how sure it must be.

Rules apply to photos added from now on. For a model you already have, run python instatagger.py <handle> --retag. Skipped photos are deleted and never downloaded again.
